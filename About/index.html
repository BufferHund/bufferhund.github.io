About Me

👋 Hey there!

I’m Zhaokun Wang, a Master’s student in Computational Linguistics at the University of Heidelberg, Germany.
I’m fascinated by how language and intelligence intersect — both in how humans express ideas and how machines try to understand them.

My current interests revolve around large language models, AI-assisted software engineering, and human–computer interaction.
I enjoy turning abstract ideas into practical systems — the kind that make developers more productive, interfaces more intuitive, and AI a little more human.

Research Direction

🧠 My long-term goal is to bridge computational linguistics and applied AI engineering.
I’m especially interested in:

  • How LLMs can assist developers through agentic reasoning and natural interfaces.
  • Multimodal fusion, integrating text, vision, and structured data for grounded understanding.
  • Efficient adaptation — LoRA, quantization, and modular fine-tuning for resource-limited environments.
