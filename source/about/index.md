### 👋 Hey there!

I’m **Zhaokun Wang**, a **Master’s candidate in Computational Linguistics (AI Track)** at **Heidelberg University**, Germany, with a background in **Computer Science and software engineering**.

My main interests are **LLM and VLM agents**, **multimodal AI**, **AI agent evaluation and safety**, and **practical AI systems**. I’m especially interested in what happens when language models move beyond text generation and begin to interact with interfaces, tools, memory, and real-world environments.

Alongside research, I enjoy building systems that connect **models, APIs, tools, and software infrastructure**. My background spans both machine learning and full-stack engineering, so I tend to approach AI problems from both a research and an engineering perspective.
